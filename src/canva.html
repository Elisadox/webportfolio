canva

I worked on this project with a classmate, Charline Rime.She was in charge of the pre-production, while I handled the creative process, including filming, developing the concept, and editing. It was one of my favorite collaborations because I was able to fully express my ideas, while she supported the organization and the production file. The assignment for this video was to create a project around the theme of intimacy, so I chose to explore the intimacy between an artist and their art. I loved working on this project because I feel it truly represents me, it is slow, takes its time to unfold, and overall creates a sense of calm and well-being.
